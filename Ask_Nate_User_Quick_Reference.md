# Meet {{AGENT_NAME}} — Your Policy Quick-Answer Assistant

**{{AGENT_NAME}}** is an AI assistant built into this SharePoint site. It reads the official **{{ORG_SHORT}} policy folder** and answers your questions about **{{AGENT_SCOPE}}** — with citations, in plain language, on demand.

Think of it as a fast first stop before you email a team or page through a 40-page standard.

---

## ✅ What {{AGENT_NAME}} Is Great At

- **"Is this allowed?"** — Quick yes/no answers grounded in our actual policies
- **"What's the rule on ___?"** — Summaries of policies, standards, and procedures
- **"Where do I find ___?"** — Pointing you to the exact document and section
- **"What should I do next?"** — Concrete next steps when a policy applies to your situation
- **"Who owns this?"** — Routing you to the right team when a human needs to take it from there

Every answer includes a **link back to the source document** so you can verify it yourself.

---

## ❌ What {{AGENT_NAME}} Won't Do

- **Guess.** If the answer isn't in the policy folder, it will say so and point you to the right team.
- **Give legal advice.** For statutory interpretation or legal questions, it will send you to Legal Counsel.
- **Handle HR matters.** Grievances, discipline, and personnel disputes go to HR.
- **Share opinions.** No politics, no commentary on people or decisions, no hot takes.
- **Process sensitive data.** Do not paste PII, credentials, or any regulated data into the chat — it will refuse and ask you to remove it.
- **Replace the experts.** For complex, novel, or high-stakes situations, go talk to a human.

---

## 💡 How to Get the Best Answers

1. **Ask naturally.** "Do I need MFA on my laptop if I'm working from home?" works better than keyword searches.
2. **Give context.** Mention the system, situation, or role if it's relevant. ("I'm setting up a new vendor integration — what's our review process?")
3. **Follow up.** If the first answer is too general, ask it to be more specific or to focus on one section.
4. **Check the references.** The agent cites real documents — click through when the answer matters.
5. **Escalate when told to.** If {{AGENT_NAME}} says *"contact [team]"*, that's the move. It's not a dodge — it means the question needs a human.

## 🔒 Safety & Privacy

- **Don't paste sensitive data.** No {{SENSITIVE_DATA_TYPES}}, passwords, keys, or anything regulated.
- **Conversations may be logged** per {{ORG_SHORT}} standard Copilot governance. Treat the chat like any other work tool.
- **{{AGENT_NAME}} only reads approved sources.** It doesn't browse the internet or pull from random documents.
